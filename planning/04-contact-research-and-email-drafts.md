## Recommended outreach sequence

1. Ask the college's SIH faculty coordinator to send or endorse the short introduction request below. Do not state that the institute has agreed to an NDA before it has.
2. Ask SIH support for the named OIL contact. Given the short timeline, a separate short routing request to OIL's corporate address is reasonable.
3. Send the technical questions to the owner once identified. If sending them to a general OIL inbox, retain the forwarding request at the start.
4. If there is no reply after about two working days, make one short call to ask who handles the request. Use the SNEH office only as an additional referral route if needed.

Do not copy a long list of unrelated officials. Old tender contacts are not confirmed owners of the SIH statement. No private employee contact details or guessed email addresses are needed.

## Draft B — Simple questions for Oil India

To: [OIL contact provided by SIH; otherwise oilindia@oilindia.in for forwarding]

Cc: [College SIH coordinator's email]

Subject: SIH26122 — Questions and sample-data request from BITS Pilani students

Dear Oil India Team,

We are a student team from BITS Pilani working on SIH26122, “Intelligent Data Capture & Schedule-Linking Layer.” If another team handles this problem, could you please forward this email to them?

We are planning a phone-friendly app where site teams can report work by voice, text, or photo. These reports would be linked to the project schedule, with unclear reports sent for review.

Before building, we would appreciate your guidance on a few questions:

1. Which Primavera product and version should we work with? For the prototype, is showing updates through an imported/exported file enough, or is a direct connection expected?
2. Are L5/L6 tasks already listed in your schedules, or are some smaller tasks kept only in site reports?
3. Who normally reports progress, and who checks it before the schedule is updated? Can any updates be accepted automatically?
4. When is a task considered finished: when the site team finishes the work, or after an inspection or approval?
5. Do reports usually include exact start and finish times, or only dates? What should happen when that information is missing?
6. Which languages should the app support, and do users need to report work without an internet connection?
7. Are there any restrictions on using online AI services to process reports, or would the system need to run on Oil India's own servers?

Could you also share a small sample schedule and a matching daily report or spreadsheet, with sensitive details removed? The statement mentions an NDA through the institute. Please let us know the process, and we will coordinate with our faculty. We are not requesting live project data.

Brief answers to any of these questions would be very helpful. If easier, we would appreciate a 15-minute call. We are preparing our prototype this week.

Thank you for your time,
[Your name]
[Team name]
BITS Pilani
[Email] | [Phone]

## Questions to save for a short call

The first email should stay manageable. Once connected to a planner or site supervisor, use these follow-ups as time permits:

- What takes the most time when you match a site report to the schedule?
- Could you show one example where a site's wording differs from the task name in the plan?
- If one spool is finished but the whole pipe line is not, how is that progress recorded?
- What happens when two reports give different dates or repeat the same work?
- How often is the schedule updated, and how quickly should new reports appear?
- What past-project information would help you plan the next project?
- What would make this prototype useful enough for your team to try?
